My Story

Hi, I’m Sam Obuya — a passionate web developer and graphics designer driven by creativity, precision, and purpose.

My journey began with a simple curiosity about how visuals and code can work together to bring ideas to life. Over time, that curiosity evolved into a deep commitment to crafting designs and digital experiences that not only look good but also connect with people.

From developing clean, functional websites to creating striking visual identities, I enjoy blending technical skill with artistic vision. Whether I’m building a dynamic web app or designing a brand’s story through visuals, I focus on clarity, impact, and user experience.

Beyond the screen, I see every project as an opportunity to learn, grow, and inspire. My goal is simple — to keep creating solutions that speak, inspire, and make a difference in the digital world.

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer? ▼

We offer a comprehensive range of services including web design and development, UI/UX design, brand identity creation, digital strategy, and ongoing maintenance. Our approach is tailored to each client's unique needs and goals.

How can I collaborate or hire you? ▼

Getting started is simple! Reach out through our contact form with details about your project. We'll schedule a complimentary consultation to discuss your vision, requirements, and timeline. From there, we'll provide a detailed proposal outlining scope, deliverables, and investment.

What tools or technologies do you use? ▼

Our tech stack includes modern frontend technologies (HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React), backend solutions (Node.js, Python), and design tools (Figma, Adobe Creative Suite). We prioritize performance, accessibility, and maintainability in all our technical decisions.

How long does it take to complete a project? ▼

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. A simple website might take 4-6 weeks, while more comprehensive projects can span 2-4 months. During our initial consultation, we'll provide a detailed timeline based on your specific requirements.

Do you provide ongoing support after project completion? ▼

Absolutely! We offer various support packages to ensure your digital presence continues to perform optimally. These include technical maintenance, content updates, performance monitoring, and strategic consultations to help your business grow.

What is your design process like? ▼

Our process follows a structured yet flexible approach: Discovery & Research, Strategy & Planning, Design & Development, Testing & Refinement, and Launch & Optimization. We maintain transparent communication throughout and welcome client feedback at every stage.

Do you work with international clients? ▼

Yes, we work with clients worldwide. Our remote collaboration process is streamlined with regular video calls, collaborative tools, and clear communication channels to ensure seamless cooperation regardless of location.
